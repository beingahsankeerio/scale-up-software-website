When our AI agents answer calls, WhatsApp messages or website chats for a client (for example a dental clinic), that client decides how callers' data is used and is the controller. If you called or messaged one of our clients, please contact that business about your data; we will help them respond.

Who we share data with

We use trusted service providers to run our business — for example website hosting, email, form handling, calendar booking, and the telephony and AI services that power our agents. They may only use the data to provide their service to us. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK; where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum to keep it protected.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become customers: up to 24 months, then deleted. Customer records: for the length of the contract plus six years, to meet tax and legal requirements. Call recordings and transcripts made for our clients are kept for the period each client sets in their agreement with us.

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

About Scale Up Softwares

Scale Up Softwares is a UK software company based in London. We build AI agents that answer, book and follow up for small and growing businesses — so no customer is left waiting for a reply.

We started from a simple observation: most small businesses don't lose customers because their service is worse. They lose them because the phone rang while everyone was busy, a WhatsApp message sat unanswered until morning, or a website visitor left without anyone to ask. Our agents close that gap.

Today we offer three services — AI voice calling agents, WhatsApp AI booking agents and website chatbots — each built around your business and set up by our team. You stay in control of what your agents do and when they hand over to a person.

Questions owners ask us

          

          

            

              What does Scale Up Softwares do?
              
We're a UK software company that builds AI agents for businesses: AI voice calling agents that answer your phone, WhatsApp AI booking agents, and website chatbots. Each one is trained on your business and set up for you.

            

            

              Which service is right for my business?
              
If most enquiries come by phone, start with an AI voice agent. If customers prefer to message, a WhatsApp booking agent fits better. If your website gets visitors who leave without contacting you, a website chatbot helps. Not sure? Book a free demo and we'll recommend one honestly.

            

            

              Do I need any technical knowledge?
              
No. Our team sets everything up — the agent's voice or tone, your services and prices, your booking rules and the connection to your number, WhatsApp, website and diary. You don't need new hardware or a new phone number.

            

            

              How much does it cost?
              
AI voice agent plans start at £99 a month with setup included — see voice agent pricing. WhatsApp booking agents and website chatbots are priced to your business; ask us for a quote. All plans are rolling monthly with no long contract.

            

            

              Will customers know they're talking to AI?
              
Yes — we recommend it, and it's how we set agents up by default. Your agent introduces itself as your virtual assistant, then gets straight to helping. Anyone who would rather speak to a person can ask for a call-back or be handed over to you, depending on the rules you set.

            

            

              How do you handle my customers' data?
              
Call recordings, transcripts, messages and booking details are used only to run your agent and send you summaries — never sold or used for marketing. Before going live we agree a data processing agreement with you, explain where data is stored and how long it's kept, and set retention to suit your business. See our privacy policy for details.
